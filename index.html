witness-kit · for any team that ships tests

Wire a mutation gate — honestly

A green suite proves your tests ran. A clean mutation gate proves your tests would notice if the code were wrong. This kit wires that proof into your CI with three rules earned across a hundred gated repos: pin the gate · read the verdict · kill or argue, never shrug — and it refuses to wire anything where the proof would be theatre.

The three honest answers

LANE A kernel/test pairs found → each proven CLEAN locally with the EXACT pinned invocation → a workflow that tees every verdict and greps '"clean": true' — anything less fails the build. You review, you push. LANE B you already mutate (Stryker & co) → belief installed; the missing piece is discipline: pin the tool, set a breaking threshold, make CI READ the score. A gate that reports and exits zero is decorative. THEATRE nothing to mutate, no mutator → REFUSED, with directions: extract one pure function with a falsifiable test first. The gate follows the kernel.

The wire proves every pair against the pinned invocation before writing a workflow — a gate is never shipped on a local approximation. Survivors block it: kill each at source, or argue it in a baseline with a sentence a reviewer could dispute. Your repo, your push.
